# Core

The widgets and themes every Flexwall wall starts with.

| Widget | Shows | Accepts |
|---|---|---|
| `stat` | One number, with an optional goal bar or the change over a series | number, series |
| `sparkline` | A number's trend with its latest value and change | series |
| `heatmap` | Daily activity over the weeks the tile fits | calendar |
| `countdown` | Days until or since a date | nothing |
| `time-left` | How much of the year, month or week is gone, as a bar or dots | nothing |
| `note` | A title and a short text | optional text |
| `link` | A card linking elsewhere, clickable on the page | nothing |

Themes: Night and Paper (free); Terminal, Sunset, Old Money and Editorial (Pro).
